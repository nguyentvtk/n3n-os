The problem

Your AI is stuck inside a chat box

You pay for AI every month, yet it is still a talented stranger.

🧠

It forgets you

ChatGPT does not remember who you are from one session to the next. Every day you explain your work from scratch.

📊

It doesn't know your numbers

Ask about today's revenue and it guesses. An AI that isn't wired into your sales, ads and calendar will always be an outsider.

💸

Real work costs extra

Agent platforms make you buy API credits and a monthly plan, while the Claude or ChatGPT plan you already have goes to waste.

The answer

Javis OS: an AI operating layer on your own machine

Javis is not a chatbot. It is an operating system for an AI assistant: it uses a full-power agent such as Claude Code as the brain, and wraps it with long-term memory, real data and working channels, on your own computer or VPS.

💰

Use the plan you already pay for

For personal use the brain runs on your own Claude or ChatGPT plan, with no API bill. For 24/7 background work, plug in an API key. Switch brains freely: Claude Code, ChatGPT, Grok, Gemini, OpenRouter and more.

🧠

Remembers you for good

A Second Brain that accumulates: living memory plus a linked Wiki. The more you use it, the better it knows you, with nothing rediscovered from scratch.

📈

Real numbers, never made up

The connection store plugs into your sales, ads, email and calendar. Javis answers with your real figures, compares them with last period and suggests next steps.

🔑

Click to connect

Paste a key, scan a QR code (Zalo) or sign in with OAuth (Google, Slack, Meta). Javis validates it right away and picks up the shop or account name itself.

🛡️

Three hard permission levels

Full access is the default: Javis places orders, runs ads, publishes posts and sends messages. To hold back one connection or one loop, lower it to Draft or Read-only, and the hub REALLY blocks it on every call.

🔀

Shared by every brain

Claude Code, ChatGPT, Grok, OpenRouter, the OpenAI or Anthropic API: all plug in through one hub. Switch brains freely and your connections stay.

  • ✓Self-hosted: local, VPS or Docker, with no data handed to any SaaS.
  • ✓Login is forced automatically when public, plus 2FA, account-takeover protection and rate limiting.

  • ✓The brain is open markdown: stop using Javis and your knowledge base stays intact.
